Service Details

Property restoration support for a range of losses.

Water & Flood Damage

Water losses can spread quickly through flooring, walls, insulation and contents. A restoration response typically begins by identifying affected areas, addressing standing water where applicable, documenting moisture conditions and developing a drying and repair plan.

Fire & Smoke Damage

Fire losses can involve structural damage, smoke residue, soot, odour and water used during firefighting. Restoration planning considers the building, finishes and contents so the required cleaning, removal and repair work is clearly defined.

Storm & Impact Damage

Wind, fallen trees and sudden impacts can affect roofs, exterior walls, windows and interior areas. The first priority is understanding the immediate risk, documenting the damage and planning temporary protection and permanent repairs.

Contents Restoration

Property losses often affect furniture, personal belongings, inventory and equipment. Contents can be assessed for cleaning, specialized treatment, storage or replacement depending on the nature and extent of the damage.

Asbestos & Mould

Mould and suspect hazardous materials require careful assessment before disturbance. Where specialized testing or remediation is needed, the work should be planned using appropriate safety and containment procedures.

Commercial Services

For commercial properties, restoration planning also considers business continuity, site access, tenants, operating hours and coordination with multiple stakeholders. Clear documentation and communication help keep the recovery organized.
